# Singular Learning Theory: notebooks

Four notebooks built to `SLT_SPECS_v1.md` (CRW's 2026-10-09 direction): the day is Bayesian statistics in thermodynamic language, plus the learning coefficient $\lambda$. Singularities get the minimum mention and nothing is organised around degeneracy. Each notebook opens with its question and a keystone reading, every section restates its notation, and the derivations are exercises with worked solutions and small CPU code cells. Nobody is expected to finish everything.

| Notebook | Topic | Page | Notebook |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Bayesian inference as statistical mechanics.** The dictionary (posterior as Boltzmann distribution, evidence as partition function, free energy, the temperature knob $\beta$) and where it is only formal; posterior concentration (the central limit theorem); Laplace's method and the BIC, the Occam factor as a volume ratio; the Bayes generalization error and the MLE-vs-Bayes comparison, with one misspecified example | [slt1_bayes_statmech.html](slt1_bayes_statmech.html) | [.ipynb](slt1_bayes_statmech.ipynb) |
| 2 | **Free energy and basin volume.** Volume scaling $V(t)\sim ct^\lambda(-\log t)^{m-1}$ and $\bar F_n\approx\lambda\log n-(m-1)\log\log n$; toy losses; $\lambda\le d/2$; the Mellin transform $\zeta(z)=\int K^z\varphi$ and its poles; several basins, the critical sample size, a one-paragraph thermodynamic reading | [slt2_free_energy_volume.html](slt2_free_energy_volume.html) | [.ipynb](slt2_free_energy_volume.ipynb) |
| 3 | **Effective exponents and temperature.** $K=w^4+\delta w^2$ and the discontinuity of $\lambda$; the effective exponent $\lambda_{\rm eff}(n)$ and its crossover at $n\sim\delta^{-2}$; **§3.2 is a marked stub for CRW's "effective theory / why temperature" argument**; the WBIC, the local learning coefficient estimator, SGLD, hyperparameters read as scales; code on the 1-D model swept over $\beta$, normal crossings, and a two-layer linear network with known $\lambda$ | [slt3_effective_temperature.html](slt3_effective_temperature.html) | [.ipynb](slt3_effective_temperature.ipynb) |
| 4 | **Applications.** A one-sentence-per-paper reading list: stagewise development, susceptibilities, Bayesian influence functions, compressibility, programs and singularities (neutral), and background for the whole day | [slt4_applications.html](slt4_applications.html) | [.ipynb](slt4_applications.ipynb) |

The pages need a network connection on first load (KaTeX from a CDN). The notebooks run on a CPU in under a minute each and need only numpy, scipy and matplotlib.

## For maintainers

- Never hand-edit the `.html` or `.ipynb` files. Each pair is generated by one builder, `build_<name>.py`, from cells defined with the helpers in `nbkit_slt.py` (`markdown()`, `reading(..., importance=, length=)`, `answer()`, `code()`, `code_answer(stub, solution)`, `references(book(...))`).
- `nbkit_slt.py` and `htmlkit_slt.py` are copies of the Training Dynamics kit (`../td/nbkit_td.py`, `../td/htmlkit_td.py`, taken 2026-10-12) with the sibling-link prefix changed from `td` to `slt`. Do not edit the TD or MDL kits from here; each day is built independently. The flags are the same: `python build_<name>.py` builds (executing the code only when it changed since the cached outputs in `_exec_cache/`, which is git-ignored); `--check` reports staleness without executing; `--rerun` forces execution; `--run` executes and times every code cell.
- `checks/slt1_checks.py`, `slt2_checks.py`, `slt3_checks.py` verify the closed forms used in the solutions numerically (`python checks/sltN_checks.py` from this directory).
- The SGLD code in Notebook 3 is the update rule of Lau et al. (2023) as implemented in Furman's `intro_lc.ipynb` (in `_sources/nb/`), rewritten in numpy; the examples follow devinterp's `normal_crossing.ipynb` and the two-layer linear network of the older worksheet, at a size that runs on a laptop. Estimates for losses with multiplicity $m=2$ come out low by the $1/\log(n\beta)$ correction, as the page says.
- Open items for CRW: §3.2 of Notebook 3 (the stub); the MacKay keystone link is the publisher's DOI (the page also links the free thesis PDF); the 2018 Watanabe book link is the publisher's DOI, which answers to browsers but not to automated fetches.
- Sources on disk: `_sources/` (the older worksheet with its LaTeX and PDF, Furman's slides, the devinterp notebooks). Exercises adapted from the worksheet: 4.1–4.2 (two basins, Notebook 2 §2.3), the regular-case volume, the cubic reparametrization, and the $\lambda\le d/2$ bound (Notebook 2 §2.1), the two-layer linear network coefficients (Notebook 3 §3.3).
