# Mysteries of Deep Learning: notebooks

Five workbooks (plus a deprecated MDL.X, kept for reference). Pick whatever interests you; the sections are largely independent, and nobody is expected to finish everything.

Each workbook is a single HTML page: open it in any browser (math is typeset by KaTeX, loaded from a CDN, so the first load needs a network connection). Every exercise is followed by its worked **solution**, folded away until you click "Solution"; hints are folded the same way. The "Show all solutions" button expands or collapses every solution at once, and printing the page prints them all. Solutions are for everyone; use them however helps you learn.

MDL4 is the 2026-10-13 merge of the former WB5 (universalities, `mdl4_universality.py`) and WB6 (the catalogue, `mdl4_catalogue.py`); anchors keep their old `wb5-`/`wb6-` ids. Each notebook opens with a **keystone** section (one or two core ideas and readings, as close to the day's mysteries as possible), then a trail of lightly annotated reading lists: each section and each reading gets about one sentence, and exercises sit in "Exercises: …" subsections. A route at the top links to its sections. In each trail section the one or two **main** readings (importance 5, heavier rule) come first; a "Supplemental" divider separates the rest. Where the unit of knowledge is a concept rather than a paper, the main reading is an encyclopedic page (Wikipedia, a tutorial, a lecture). Every reading carries ARENA-style scores, **Importance** and **Length** (1–5 dots each; the key is under the contents). Books are not scored: they are listed as reference links at the bottom of a reading list. Sections and subsections are folded; open what you want, or use "Expand all sections". The interactive figures are self-contained HTML files in [`assets/interactive/`](assets/interactive/README.md).

## For maintainers

- Never hand-edit the `.html` files. Each is generated from one builder: `build_mdl1.py` … `build_mdl4.py`, `build_mdlX.py`. MDL1's cells live in per-section modules listed in `build_mdl1.py` (`wb2_keystone.py`, `wb2_classical.py`, `wb2_regularization.py`, `wb2_interpolation.py`, `wb2_pacbayes.py`, `wb2_bayes_sgd.py`, `wb2_ib.py`). The pre-restructure builders and pages (2026-10-09, before the keystone/trail format) are in `_pre_trail_2026-10-09/`; older ones in `_old_wb2/`, `_old_wb5/`.
- Build with `python build_mdlN.py` (needs `pandoc` on PATH; it converts each cell's Markdown, including `$…$`/`$$…$$` math, to HTML at build time). Use `--check` to validate a builder and report whether its notebook is stale; it changes nothing on disk.
- Shared helpers are in `nbkit.py`: `reading(..., importance=, length=)` requires both scores (1–5); books go in `references(book(...), ...)` at the end of a list; `supplemental()` inserts the main/supplemental divider; `markdown()` cells become the page; every `##`/`###`/`####` section is folded into a collapsed `<details class="sec">` at render time; each `answer()` cell becomes a collapsed "Solution" block after the cell before it, and `<details><summary>Hint</summary>` blocks in Markdown become folded hints. The page template (CSS, contents sidebar, KaTeX loader) is at the bottom of `nbkit.py`. The build fails on unbalanced `<details>`, TeX left outside math, missing local link targets, and dangling `#anchors`.
- The older exercises/solutions `.ipynb` pairs are kept, unmaintained, in `_old_ipynb/`.
- `python checks/escape_check.py build_mdl*.py wb2_*.py mdl4_*.py` flags TeX eaten by Python string escapes (e.g. `"\times"` in a non-raw string). Numerical checks for MDL1 are in `checks/` (`wb2a_check.py`, `wb2b_check.py`, `wb2_new_*_check.py`; they use the pre-restructure exercise numbering).
- Current format: [`../reviews/2026-10-09-trail-restructure-brief.md`](../reviews/2026-10-09-trail-restructure-brief.md), which overrides the older [`2026-10-08-editorial-brief.md`](../reviews/2026-10-08-editorial-brief.md) where they conflict. The per-workbook review records are in the same directory. Older workbook specs are historical and do not override the brief.
