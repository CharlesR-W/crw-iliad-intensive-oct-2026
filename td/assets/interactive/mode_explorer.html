Mode explorer: Saxe dynamics with imbalance and depth

Each singular mode of the target Σyx = Σα sα uαvα⊤ evolves on its own under gradient flow from decoupled initial conditions. Small, balanced initialization gives sigmoids ordered by s, with plateaus. Large imbalance between layers (d ≫ s and a₀) removes the plateaus and makes every mode relax exponentially at a nearly common rate (close to d for two layers, larger for deeper ones); intermediate d gives a mix. For small a₀, extra depth lengthens the plateaus.
