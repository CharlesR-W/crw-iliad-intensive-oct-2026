Steepest descent under four norms

To first order a step Δ changes the loss by ⟨g, Δ⟩. The steepest-descent step of size η is the point of the norm ball of radius η that lies furthest along −g. Change the ball and the direction changes: the Euclidean ball gives −g, the ℓ∞ box gives −sign(g), the ℓ1 diamond gives a single coordinate, and an ellipse (a Mahalanobis or Fisher norm) gives −M−1g.
